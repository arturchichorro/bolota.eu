During election campaing, candidates attempt to shape voters' belifes through ads, media engagement, etc
Studies have consistently found that LLMs are able to write messages that persuade on political issues. Different studies have found different results though: messaging crafted by AI was more persuasive than human written content in some studies, but in others it was roughly human level. LLMs greatest potential impact right now seems to be the ability to cheaply and at scale generate this messaging. Given the pace of research in the field, the picture may change very fast.

Another capacity of LLMs: political microtargeting: tailoring political messages to a specific individual based on features such as demographic or social media activigty. Cambridge Analytica was the first example of this, 50 million Facebook users 2016 US presidential election and UK Brexit Rferendum scraped data for targeted political ads. LLMs could distort campaignign by mass producing hihgly tailored messages with minimal human intervention. Studies have found that microtargeted messages are rarely more effective than the single most persuasive message across the entire population

Multimodal Gen AI has greatly expanded opportunies for malicious actors to create and manipulate digital content. Producing deceptive campaign videos, portray political rivals in compromising or defamatory wats. Counterfeiting entire news websites. Hyper realist generative content could threaten to rob news media of its epistemic backstop

### Capacity to be Persuasive, NotebookLM

One of my favorite AI tools that I actually use is NotebookLM, by Google. It allows you to upload PDFs, Documents, notes, or use websites, that it then uses to create a personalised AI "notebook" that summarises key points, and where you can ask questions related to the contents of the documents. But maybe the coolest feature it has, is the possibility to generate both Audio and Video overviews about the documents. (basically a podcast or a presentation). Here are examples of that:

(exemplos)

I've used this extensively to learn about all kinds of things, specifically about AI and the direction of its development, multiple research papers about AI techniques, social impacts of AI, Disinformation and how it happens, etc. It's an incredibly usefull tool. But what makes it truly incredible is not necessarily it's ability to summarise documents, or reply to questions about the documents. It is the fact that it makes these documents into something digestible: it frames them in the right format (podcast, video, etc), and makes them easier to consume and know about. And this is extremely powerfull, because us humans easily get bored.
